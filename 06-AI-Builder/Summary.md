# Microsoft Copilot Studio

## What is Copilot Studio?

Microsoft Copilot Studio is a **low-code AI platform** that enables users to build intelligent conversational agents (copilots) without advanced AI or programming expertise.

These agents can understand natural language, answer questions, retrieve business data, automate tasks, and integrate with Microsoft Power Platform and Microsoft 365 services.

---

## Business Value

Copilot Studio helps organizations:

- Provide instant, 24/7 support for employees and customers.
- Reduce repetitive support requests.
- Improve productivity through self-service.
- Automate business processes.
- Deliver consistent and accurate information.
- Enhance decision-making with AI-powered assistance.

---

## Key Features

### AI-Powered Agents

Create intelligent agents that can:

- Understand natural language.
- Hold multi-turn conversations.
- Retrieve business information.
- Perform business actions.
- Escalate to human agents when needed.

---

### Knowledge Sources

Agents can access information from:

- SharePoint
- Websites
- Microsoft Dataverse
- Custom knowledge bases

---

### Automation

Agents integrate with **Power Automate** to:

- Create support tickets
- Send notifications
- Trigger approvals
- Update records
- Book appointments

---

### Deployment Channels

Agents can be deployed to:

- Microsoft Teams
- Power Pages
- SharePoint
- Mobile Apps
- Microsoft 365 Copilot
- Custom Applications

---

# Copilots and Generative AI in Microsoft Power Platform

## What is Generative AI?

Generative AI is a type of artificial intelligence that creates new content—such as text, data models, code, reports, and websites—based on natural language instructions.

Instead of manually building every component, users simply describe what they need, and AI generates a working solution that can be refined further.

---

## Business Value

Generative AI and Copilot help organizations:

- Build solutions faster.
- Reduce manual work.
- Improve productivity.
- Simplify app and workflow development.
- Make Power Platform accessible to non-developers.
- Enhance decision-making with AI-generated insights.

---

## Copilot Across Power Platform

### Power Apps

- Generates apps from natural language.
- Creates Dataverse tables.
- Builds screens and navigation.

### Power Automate

- Creates workflows from descriptions.
- Suggests connectors and automation steps.
- Explains existing flows in plain language.

### Power Pages

- Generates websites and page layouts.
- Creates forms and Dataverse tables.
- Produces website content.

### Power BI

- Builds reports and dashboards.
- Creates charts from natural language.
- Answers questions about business data.

### Microsoft Dataverse

- Suggests tables and relationships.
- Designs data models.
- Organizes imported data.

---

## AI Builder

AI Builder enables users to add AI capabilities without coding.

Common features include:

- Document processing
- Image recognition
- Text classification
- Predictive analytics

Example:
Automatically extracting information from invoices to eliminate manual data entry.
