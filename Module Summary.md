# Microsoft Power Platform Overview

The following summarizes the key products and capabilities of **Microsoft Power Platform**:

## Power Apps

**Power Apps** is a low-code application development platform for building:

- Canvas Apps
- Model-driven Apps
- Mobile applications

It enables users of all skill levels to create custom business applications that connect to hundreds of data sources, including **Microsoft Dataverse**, with minimal coding.

---

## Power Automate

**Power Automate** enables organizations to automate business processes and repetitive tasks.

It supports:

- Automated workflows
- Approval processes
- Document processing
- System integration

Power Automate includes:

- **Cloud Flows** – Triggered by events or schedules.
- **Desktop Flows** – Robotic Process Automation (RPA) for legacy desktop applications.

---

## Power Pages

**Power Pages** is a low-code platform for building secure, external-facing business websites.

Common use cases include:

- Customer portals
- Supplier portals
- Government service portals
- Public business websites

Power Pages integrates seamlessly with **Microsoft Dataverse** and the rest of the Power Platform.

---

## Microsoft Dataverse

**Microsoft Dataverse** is the cloud-based data platform that serves as the foundation of Power Platform.

It provides:

- Secure data storage
- Scalable business data management
- Built-in business logic
- Role-based security
- Integration with:
  - Microsoft 365
  - Azure
  - Dynamics 365
  - Third-party services

---

## Copilot Studio

**Copilot Studio** is a low-code platform for creating intelligent AI agents.

These agents can:

- Answer questions
- Execute business actions
- Automate tasks
- Operate autonomously

Agents can be deployed to:

- Microsoft Teams
- Power Pages
- Other supported channels

They also integrate with Power Platform data and workflows.

---

## Copilots and Generative AI

Generative AI is integrated throughout Microsoft Power Platform.

Copilot helps users generate:

- Apps
- Workflows
- Data models
- Websites
- Reports

using natural language prompts.

**AI Builder** extends these capabilities with AI models for:

- Document processing
- Object detection
- Predictive analytics

---

## Connectors

**Connectors** provide the integration layer for Power Platform.

They offer pre-built connections to **1,800+ services**, allowing Power Apps and Power Automate to:

- Exchange data
- Trigger actions
- Connect Microsoft services
- Connect enterprise applications
- Connect third-party platforms

without requiring custom API development.

---

## Plan Designer

**Plan Designer** is an AI-powered workspace within Power Apps that transforms a business problem into a complete Power Platform solution.

It helps identify:

- User roles
- Business processes
- Required applications
- Automated workflows
- AI agents
- Power BI reports
- Dataverse tables

Plan Designer generates working starting points for a complete, end-to-end solution, helping organizations accelerate solution design and development.
