# Plan Designer

## What is Plan Designer?

Plan Designer is an **AI-powered workspace** in **Microsoft Power Apps** that transforms a business problem into a complete **Power Platform solution plan** using natural language.

Instead of starting from scratch, users describe their business requirements, and AI agents generate a solution blueprint that includes apps, workflows, data models, reports, websites, and AI agents.

---

## Business Value

Plan Designer helps organizations:

- Accelerate solution planning and development.
- Reduce time spent on solution design.
- Improve collaboration between business and IT teams.
- Generate complete end-to-end Power Platform solutions.
- Ensure solutions are well-structured and scalable.
- Minimize manual planning and design effort.

---

## AI Agents in Plan Designer

### Solution Agent

Designs the overall Power Platform solution and ensures all components work together.

### Process Agent

Maps business processes and identifies opportunities for automation using Power Automate and Copilot Studio.

### Data Agent

Designs the Microsoft Dataverse data model, including tables, columns, and relationships.

### Experience Agent

Creates role-based user experiences by recommending Canvas Apps, Model-driven Apps, Power Pages sites, and Power BI reports.

---

## Components Generated

Plan Designer can automatically generate:

- Canvas Apps
- Model-driven Apps
- Power Automate Flows
- Copilot Studio Agents
- Power Pages Websites
- Power BI Reports
- Microsoft Dataverse Tables

---

## How It Works

1. Describe the business problem in natural language.
2. AI agents analyze the requirements.
3. A complete solution blueprint is created.
4. Review and refine the proposed solution.
5. Generate working Power Platform components.
